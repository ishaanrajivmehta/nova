Senior Clinical Research Associate
You will monitor oncology trial sites to good clinical practice, performing source data verification
and managing the trial master file and investigator site file. Experience with electronic data capture
systems (Medidata Rave or Veeva Vault) and a clinical trial management system essential. You will handle
adverse event reporting and serious adverse event escalation, manage protocol deviation documentation,
and lead site initiation visit activity. Knowledge of 21 cfr part 11 and MHRA inspection readiness required.
