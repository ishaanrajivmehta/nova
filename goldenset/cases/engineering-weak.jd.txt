Structural Engineer (Buildings)
We seek a Structural Engineer for commercial building projects. You will deliver reinforced concrete design
and structural steel design to Eurocode, produce models in Revit and Tekla Structures, and run analysis
in Etabs or Staad Pro. Experience with foundation design, geotechnical investigation and temporary works
design required. Familiarity with CDM regulations, NEC contract and JCT contract administration essential.
Working towards chartered engineer status with IStructE. Building information modelling experience desirable.
